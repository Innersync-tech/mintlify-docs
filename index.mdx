## Get up and running

Follow these three steps to go from zero to a fully configured Alphapy server.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Alphapy to your server">
    Visit [pricing.alphapy.innersync.tech](https://pricing.alphapy.innersync.tech) and click the **Add to Discord** invite link. Authorize the bot with the requested permissions — Alphapy needs `Manage Channels`, `Manage Roles`, `Send Messages`, `Read Message History`, and `Moderate Members` to operate all features.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the setup wizard">
    Once Alphapy is in your server, run `/config start` in any channel you have admin access to. The interactive wizard walks you through every major setting — log channel, rules channel, onboarding flow, embed watcher, invites, GDPR agreement flow, ticket category, and staff role — one step at a time. Click **Skip** on any step you want to configure later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Innersync account">
    Run `/link` to connect your Discord account to your Innersync ID. This unlocks the AI reflection agents (`/agent`) and syncs your profile across the Alphapy bot, the Innersync App dashboard, and Core. Members can each run `/link` independently from within your server.
  </Step>
</Steps>
